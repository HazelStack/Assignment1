About Me

I am a first-generation college graduate from the University of California, Santa Barbara, where I earned a Bachelor's degree in Environmental Studies. I am passionate about community-centered sustainability and actively participate in local cleanups, community gardens, and supporting small, local businesses. Currently, I am expanding my skills in software engineering, with the goal of merging technology and environmental advocacy. I aim to create tools and platforms that protect both people and the planet, especially in communities that need it most.

My Hobbies

  • Gardening: Growing native plants to support pollinators and local ecosystems.
  • Photography: Capturing the beauty of both natural and urban environments.
  • Watercolor Painting: A relaxing way to stay mindful and creative.

My Goals

  1. Become a Software Engineer working on projects that create a positive social and environmental impact.
  2. Establish a nonprofit organization dedicated to protecting marginalized communities from environmental hazards.
  3. Develop technology solutions that connect environmental justice with innovative tools and platforms.
